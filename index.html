About me

I'd rather ship it than pitch it

I like turning an idea into something people can actually click on. That instinct started with iDelivery, a hyperlocal delivery platform I founded while still a student, and it's carried through into most of what I build now: analytics tools, MLOps demos, and small business tools that go from a notebook or a rough idea to a deployed, working app.

A good example is my World Cup 2026 analytics project — it began as a Google Colab notebook with a Random Forest model, and grew into a full Dixon-Coles Poisson goal model with calibrated probabilities, a Streamlit dashboard, CI/CD, and a LangChain + Gemini agent that answers questions over the match data.

I'm entrepreneurially minded by default: I'd rather learn a tool by shipping something with it than by reading about it. Most of what I know about large language models, RAG pipelines, and API integration, I learned by building products that needed them.
